## Physics concept of the day: snapshots per orbit ($N$)

**ML bridge.** $N$ is the sampling rate of a time series. Changing it from 64 to 128 is like doubling the frame rate of a video: each frame changes less, and a fixed context window covers half as much of the story.

**Physics.** Each orbit takes one period $P$, the time to go around once. The lab samples $N$ snapshots per orbit, so the time step is $\Delta t = P/N$. The model predicts the next position from the change between snapshots, and the part of that change due to gravity scales as $\Delta t^2$:

$$a_{\text{step}} \propto \left(\tfrac{2\pi}{N}\right)^2$$

Doubling $N$ therefore makes the per-step gravitational "kick" **four times smaller**. With context_len 128, an N=128 model also sees exactly one orbit per window, while an N=64 model sees two.

**Tie-back.** This is why `exp-20261016-084530-ef04` exists. If seeing only one orbit per window explains the 0.071 force gap in `f-…dd1c`, then giving N=128 a 256-step context should close most of it. The lab hasn't yet tested *why* the deficit exists; the context window is one candidate explanation.
